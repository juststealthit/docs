# FAQ

## Launching

**Can the creator mint more coins or pull the liquidity?**
No. The supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 with no way to mint more, and the creator has no way to change the tax or launch type after launch. On Raydium LaunchLab and Meteora the liquidity is locked forever at graduation, and on EVM chains from launch. On Pump.fun, Pump's own program runs graduation.

**Which venue should I pick?**
Pump.fun pairs with SOL or what Pump lists and pays Pump's own creator fee. Raydium LaunchLab has the most pairs and five tax tiers. Meteora has the same rates, 20 shared pairs plus other tokens you search for, and an anti-sniper fee at launch. EVM chains have the same rates, pair with their native coin and have the anti-sniper fee too. See [Venues](venues.md).

**Can I launch a coin paired with a stock?**
Yes, on Raydium LaunchLab, or on Pump.fun where Pump lists one. Choose the Holders launch type too and your holders are paid in that stock. Tokenized stocks aren't launchable on Meteora. Tokenized stocks are not for US persons.

**Is a multi-chain launch one coin?**
No. Each chain gets a separate coin with its own supply and price. They aren't bridged, and some chains can land while others fail.

**Can I get a launch refunded?**
No. A launch that landed can't be undone or refunded, and neither can its costs. A multi-chain launch returns money it never spent.

## Fees and payouts

**Does "100% of creator fees" mean 100% of the tax?**
No. Creator fees are the coin's part of the fee. See [Fees](fees.md).

**When do holders get paid?**
On Holders coins, a round runs every 10 minutes. Wallets holding 10,000+ tokens are paid in the coin's pair token, and very small amounts wait for a later round. Your payouts show under Earnings in your Wallet page.

**How does Buy back and burn work?**
Each time a coin's fees are claimed, they buy the coin and burn what they bought. It needs about 0.006 SOL in the escrow to start, keeps a small reserve for fees, and limits each buy by price impact and size. The rest waits for the next claim. A burn isn't a promise of any price or value.

**Why is the Raydium coin share smaller at high tiers?**
Raydium caps the creator fee at 0.5% on the curve. Higher tiers add only to Just Stealth It's share.

**Why haven't my fees from another chain arrived?**
Routing fees home is built and currently off. They wait in that chain's pot until it's on. See [Fees across chains](multi-chain.md#fees-across-chains).

## Perps and staking

**Does Just Stealth It hold my perps positions?**
No. Positions are held by the venue: Hyperliquid or Jupiter. You trade from your own wallet. See [Perps](perps.md).

**Can I trade Aster here?**
Not yet. Aster's markets and charts are live, and trading is coming soon.

**Is staking open?**
Not yet. It's built and tested, and opens soon. Rates are announced at launch. See [Staking](staking.md).

**What is stSTEALTH?**
The receipt you get 1:1 when you stake $STEALTH. You hand it back to unstake, so keep it in the wallet that staked.

**How long does unstaking take?**
Your $STEALTH comes back 6 hours after you hand back stSTEALTH, with its rewards.

No. It's coming soon, with the Stealth AMM. Solana coins launch on Pump.fun, Raydium LaunchLab and Meteora today. See [Stealth BC and Stealth AMM](stealth-bc-amm.md).

**When do Avalanche, Arbitrum and X Layer open?**
They show as coming soon. There's no date.

## Wallets and support

**Which wallets work?**
On Solana: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and other Solana wallets. On EVM chains: the wallet in your browser.

**Did the person named on a coin agree to it?**
No. A launcher can name any public account. That person hasn't agreed to anything and doesn't endorse the coin unless they say so themselves. Fees wait in a vault until they claim.

**A coin disappeared from the list.**
Moderators can hide reported coins from the lists and search. The token page still loads with a notice, and the coin keeps trading on chain.

**Is Just Stealth It part of Pump.fun, Raydium or Meteora?**
No. Coins launch and trade on those programs through Just Stealth It's own launch flow. It's built on them and independently operated. It isn't an official Pump.fun, Raydium, Meteora or Solana product.

**Are the contracts audited?**
No. See [Safety and trust](safety.md#audit).

**How do I reach the team?**
Through the form on [juststealthit.xyz/status](https://juststealthit.xyz/status). Automated accounts on X and Telegram aren't support. Nobody from Just Stealth It will ever ask for your recovery phrase.

**Is something down?**
The [status page](https://juststealthit.xyz/status) shows live systems, uptime and incidents.

**Is any of this financial advice?**
No. Coins launched here are created by anyone, and you can lose everything you put in. Nothing here promises a return. Trade at your own risk.
