# Telegram and X

The full guide to every bot, the group's topics and the X account is in [juststealthit/Stealth-Bots](https://github.com/juststealthit/Stealth-Bots).

## The group

Real-time launches and events post to [t.me/juststealthit](https://t.me/juststealthit), by topic.

## X

Launches aren't posted on X. The [X account](https://x.com/juststealthit) posts platform updates and memes, and the Stealth agent replies to questions about coins.

Some of that text and those images are generated by the Stealth agent and can be wrong. Check the transaction and the coin's page. Neither X nor Telegram is a support channel.

## The private chat

> Built and **currently off**. This describes how it works once it's switched on.

The bot's private chat is a launch terminal and a wallet. It launches on Solana with buttons, using the same launch service as the site, and shows the most a launch can cost before you confirm. EVM chains show Coming soon there.

**Just Stealth It holds this wallet's key** and signs when you confirm a launch or a withdrawal. Keep there only what you plan to use.

- You get one Solana wallet per Telegram user.
- Withdrawals send SOL. Tokens from a first buy stay in the wallet.
- You can export the wallet's key in the chat. It arrives as a message that deletes itself after about a minute, and whoever has it controls the wallet.

Launches, withdrawals and key exports have limits per user, by the hour and by the day, and launches and withdrawals also have a daily spending limit. All users together have a daily ceiling too. The chat's help screen shows the current limits.
