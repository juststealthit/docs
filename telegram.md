# Telegram

## The group

Real-time launches and events post to [t.me/juststealthit](https://t.me/juststealthit), by topic.

Automated accounts also post launches and events on [X](https://x.com/juststealthit), and an X account answers questions. Some of that text and those images come from AI models and can be wrong. They aren't a support channel.

## The private chat

> Built and **currently off**. This describes how it works once it's switched on.

The bot's private chat is a launch terminal and a wallet. It launches on Solana with buttons, using the same launch service as the site, and shows the most a launch can cost before you confirm. EVM chains show Coming soon there.

**Just Stealth It holds this wallet's key** and signs when you confirm a launch or a withdrawal. Keep there only what you plan to use.

- You get one Solana wallet per Telegram user.
- Withdrawals send SOL. Tokens from a first buy stay in the wallet.
- You can export the wallet's key in the chat. It arrives as a message that deletes itself after about 60 seconds, and whoever has it controls the wallet.

| Default limit | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Launches | 5 an hour |
| Withdrawals | 5 an hour |
| Key exports | 3 a day |
| Launches, rolling 24 hours | 25 SOL |
| Withdrawals, rolling 24 hours | 25 SOL |

All users together also have a daily ceiling.
