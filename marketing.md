# Marketing ladder

Marketing is a launch type. On a Marketing coin, 100% of creator fees go into the coin's **marketing pot**. The creator takes none. A Mix can give marketing a slice instead.

## How the pot works

The pot is credited in US dollars at the moment each fee is earned. It climbs a ladder of promotions, one step at a time, in order.

1. A step is **ready** once the pot covers its price plus $5. The buffer covers price moves between the unlock and the purchase.
2. An admin picks it up, so it shows as **processing**.
3. The admin buys it and records what it really cost, with proof. It then shows as **completed**.

A step bought below or above its list price moves the next one. Only the first step not bought yet can be ready. Later ones wait.

The steps are bought by people, not by a contract. The token page shows the ladder's progress.
