# Perps

Perps is the trading section for perpetuals and spot, at [juststealthit.xyz/perps](https://juststealthit.xyz/perps). It shows markets from three venues side by side. You trade on the venue itself, from your own wallet.

| Venue | Chain | Markets | Trading here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hyperliquid** | HyperCore | Every listed perp, and spot pairs | **On**, from an EVM wallet |
| **Jupiter Perps** | Solana | SOL, ETH and BTC perps | **On**, from a Solana wallet |
| **Aster** | BNB Chain | USDT perps, including some stocks and commodities | Markets and charts now, trading coming soon |

The full guide is in [juststealthit/Perps](https://github.com/juststealthit/Perps): the order ticket, positions, deposits, charts and phones.

## Who holds what

Your positions are held by the venue, not by Just Stealth It. Orders are signed by your wallet, or by a trading key your wallet approved in your own browser.
