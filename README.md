# Just Stealth It docs

Just Stealth It is a multi-chain launchpad and trading terminal. Launch on every chain. Earn from one place.

On **Solana** a coin launches on **Pump.fun**, **Raydium LaunchLab** or **Meteora**. It can also launch on **Robinhood Chain**, **BNB Chain**, **HyperEVM**, **Base** or **Arc**. **Avalanche**, **Arbitrum** and **X Layer** show as coming soon. Every buy and sell pays a fee. The coin's part of that fee is called **creator fees**, and the coin's **launch type** decides where they go.
