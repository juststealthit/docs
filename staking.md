# Staking

> Built, tested and **opening soon**. This page describes how it works once it opens. Rates are announced at launch.

Stake **$STEALTH**, hold a receipt, and earn $STEALTH while it's staked. Staking runs on Solana, from a Solana wallet.

## Where it is

Open **Wallet**, then the **Stake** tab. On phones it's under **More**. It lists the pools, one coin per card, with its rate, total staked, stakers and the reward pool. $STEALTH is the first pool. More coins may follow. Until staking opens, the tab says so.

A pool's page shows:

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Rate | The pool's yearly rate, and the boosts |
| Your position | What you have staked, rewards earned, stSTEALTH in your wallet, and your boost with the date of the next one |
| Stake and Unstake | The form for each |
| Unstaking | Each unstake on its way back, with a countdown |
| What you could earn | An estimate for an amount over a number of days |
| History | Your stakes, unstakes and payouts, with their transactions |

## How it works

1. **Stake.** Your $STEALTH goes into an escrow wallet of your own, and you get the same amount of **stSTEALTH**, 1:1. stSTEALTH is the receipt for your stake.
2. **Earn.** Rewards build up every second you stay staked, in $STEALTH, at the pool's published rate.
3. **Boost.** The rate rises in steps the longer your stake stays open. The steps count from your first stake.
4. **Unstake.** Hand back stSTEALTH. Your $STEALTH comes back **6 hours** later, with its share of the rewards, by itself.

Coins on their way back don't earn. A partial unstake takes its share of the rewards earned so far, and the rest stays with the stake.

## The escrow deposit

Your first stake also puts a small SOL deposit into your escrow. It pays the network fees of the payouts. It comes back to you when you've unstaked everything and the escrow closes. The form shows the amount before you sign.

## Keep your stSTEALTH

You need stSTEALTH in the same wallet that staked to unstake. Without it you can't unstake. Sending stSTEALTH to another wallet doesn't hand your stake to anyone.

## Rewards

Rewards are paid in $STEALTH from a reward pool, at the rate the pool shows. The estimate on the page uses today's rate and is not a promise. The rate can change, and rewards stop while the reward pool is empty. Staked coins aren't touched by that, and rewards resume when the pool is refilled.

## Trust

Staking works like the platform's other escrows. **Just Stealth It holds the key of each staking escrow**, so it can move what's in one. A stake is credited only once the chain confirms it, and a payout runs only after stSTEALTH is handed back. See [Safety and trust](safety.md#keys-it-holds).

Staking isn't audited. Nothing here is financial advice or a promise of any return.
