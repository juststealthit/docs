# Getting started

## Three ways in

**Connect a wallet.** Open **Connect** in the top bar, pick a chain, then a wallet. On Solana that's Phantom, Solflare, Backpack or another Solana wallet. On an EVM chain it's the wallet in your browser. Avalanche, Arbitrum and X Layer show there as coming soon. Just Stealth It never sees that wallet's keys, and you sign your own transactions. You need a little of the chain's coin for network fees.

**Sign in with a social account.** This lets you post in the Feed or claim a [Social vault](social-vaults.md). Clerk runs the sign-in. The buttons shown depend on which logins are enabled.

**Use the Telegram bot.** Its private chat is built and currently off. See [Telegram and X](telegram.md).

## Wallet and network status

Once connected, tap your wallet in the top bar. It shows your address, balance and shortcuts, plus live network status: the latest block, your latency to the API, and how close the indexer is to the chain.

| Dot | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Everything is in sync |
| Amber | The indexer lags |
| Red | It's far behind, or the connection dropped |

## Trade settings

The gear in the trade panel holds your maximum slippage, buy presets, the quick-buy amount on the coin list, and sell percentages. They're saved in this browser only.

## Where things are

| Page | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Feed** | What's happening across every chain |
| **Launch** | The launch form |
| **Coins** | Every coin at every stage: New, Graduating and Graduated. Sort, filter by chain, or buy from a row |
| **Perps** | Perpetuals and spot on Hyperliquid, Jupiter and Aster. See [Perps](perps.md) |
| **Wallet** | Your Portfolio: holdings and PnL, trades, earnings, CTO applications, the coins you launched, and Stake |
| **Token page** | Chart, trade panel, Activity, Holders, Dev, and the coin's details. On phones, the eye in the header opens the details |
| **Fees** | Creator fees across every chain and coin, the dev wallet's buys and the official coin's burns |
| **Status** | Live systems, uptime, incidents and the support form |

## Wallet

The Wallet page is your Portfolio. Without a wallet it shows its outline at zero, and the **Connect** button in the top bar is the one way in.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| The top | Net worth with its PnL for the period, and Deposit, Launch or Withdraw, and Swap |
| Holdings | What you hold, with value and PnL |
| Two price cards | The coins you hold most of, then the busiest coins here |
| Earnings | Creator fees waiting and holder rewards |
| Activity | Your trades for the period |
| Perps | Your Perps account. See [Perps](perps.md) |
| Sub wallets | Your Solana wallet and your sub wallet on the EVM chains |
| More | History, CTO, [Stake](staking.md), and a referral link that's coming soon |
| Top movers | The biggest gainers and losers |

From a computer the same pages are tabs along the top: Overview, Holding, Activity, Earnings, History, CTO and Stake. On phones each one opens from its card.

The site is in English, 简体中文, 日本語, Español and Français.

## Your first coin

1. Connect a wallet with enough to pay for the launch.
2. Open **Launch** and fill in name, ticker, logo, description and links.
3. Pick where it launches and what it pairs with.
4. Pick the tax, where the venue has a choice.
5. Pick the [launch type](launch-types.md). It's locked once you launch.
6. Add a first buy if you want one.
7. Review and sign.

The full walkthrough is in [Launching a coin](launching.md).
