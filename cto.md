# CTO (community takeover)

If a creator walks away, holders who keep the coin alive can apply to receive its creator fees. This is a **CTO**. It matters on coins whose escrow pays a creator.

## Applying

You need:

1. A connected wallet holding the amount of the coin the form asks for.
2. A verification post on X with the exact text the form gives you, a post showing your work for the coin, and a few community links.
3. A few answers about the creator, the community and your plans, the wallet that should receive the fees, and a signature. It's a message, not a transaction: nothing moves.

Apply from the CTO link at the bottom of the coin's details. The team reviews every application by hand. Follow its status in your Wallet page.

## Notice and what moves

1. If an application is approved, the token page announces the change with a 24-hour notice.
2. During the notice the change can be cancelled, for example if the creator turns out to be active.
3. After the notice, the coin's creator fees go to the new wallet.

**The backlog stays with the old creator.** A CTO never moves fees earned before it takes effect.

On EVM chains a takeover also needs the new creator to accept it, and the admin can cancel it.
