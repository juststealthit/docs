# The Feed

The Feed shows what's happening across every chain: platform posts made by software from on-chain events, and what people post. Its Fees tab shows payouts.

## Posting

To post, follow or report, prove a wallet with a signature or sign in with a verified social account. A profile page shows someone's posts and follows.

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Post length | 500 characters |
| Links | 3, https only |
| Images | 1 |
| HTML | None |

There are rate limits, and some link hosts are blocked. An uploaded image can stay on IPFS even after you delete the post.

## Trending

Trending lists the coins moving right now: ours and [external coins](external-tokens.md) from every chain, Solana first. $STEALTH always sits in the top 5. Each coin shows its chain and where it comes from: the Stealth logo for coins launched here, the launchpad's logo for external ones.

## On desktop

The Feed opens as three columns side by side. Each column picks what it shows from its header: the timeline, coins, posts, socials, Trending, top launchers or fees. Or switch to one wide column with a fuller side rail. Your choice is remembered in this browser.

## Moderation

Use Report to flag a post. Moderators can hide, show or remove a post and ban a profile. Hiding a coin takes its platform posts and any post naming its address with it.

Posts are their authors' views, and platform posts are automated. None of it is advice.
