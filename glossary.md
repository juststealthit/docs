# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Anti-sniper fee** | 90% at launch, stepping down every second to the coin's tax over 15 seconds, on Meteora and EVM launches |
| **Bonding curve** | The pricing a coin trades on before it graduates. On EVM chains, the shape of its locked positions |
| **Buy back and burn** | A launch type: creator fees buy the coin and burn it on each claim. A burn isn't a promise of any price or value |
| **Creator fees** | The coin's part of the fee. 100% of creator fees go where the launch type says. That never means 100% of the tax |
| **CTO** | Community takeover: holders apply to receive a coin's creator fees after a public notice |
| **Distributor** | The ops bot's role in the EVM contracts. It pays pots and runs buy-backs |
| **Escrow** | The coin's own account that creator fees land in. Just Stealth It controls it. On EVM chains it's the fee vault |
| **Fee vault** | The EVM contract that books every fee |
| **Global tax** | A Token-2022 transfer fee on a Meteora coin. Built and off |
| **Graduation** | When the curve sells out and the coin moves to a pool. On EVM chains, a price level |
| **Hidden wallet** | An EVM wallet Just Stealth It holds for your Solana wallet. Same address on every EVM chain. Also called a sub wallet |
| **Holder rewards** | On a Holders coin, wallets holding 10,000+ tokens are paid every 10 minutes in the pair token. Very small amounts carry over |
| **Home** | On a multi-chain launch, the chain that pays |
| **Launch type** | Where a coin's creator fees go. Picked at launch and locked |
| **Marketing ladder** | The 21 ordered promotions a Marketing coin's pot pays for |
| **Marketing wallet** | The public wallet that receives the marketing share and pays for the promotions on a ladder |
| **Mix** | A launch type that splits creator fees across 2 to 5 slices |
| **Multi-chain launch** | One payment that makes a separate coin on each chain |
| **Pair** | What a coin trades against. Set at launch |
| **Pot** | Money a coin keeps to spend on its launch type, such as holder rounds |
| **Relay** | The bridge that moves money between chains |
| **Singular** | A launch that makes one coin, where you picked |
| **Social vault** | One stacked vault per named account, where creator fees wait until that person claims |
| **Tax tier** | The tax every trade pays, set at launch: 1.25% to 5.75% on Raydium LaunchLab, Meteora and EVM chains. Pump.fun sets its own fee |
| **Team** | 2 to 10 wallets that split a Team coin's creator fees, by shares set at launch |
| **Treasury** | Just Stealth It's share of every coin's fee |
| **Venue** | Where a coin launches: Pump.fun, Raydium LaunchLab, Meteora, Robinhood Chain, BNB Chain, HyperEVM, Base or Arc |
