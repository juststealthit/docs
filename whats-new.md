# What's new

We keep a running list of what changed on Just Stealth It, so it's clear what you're using. Newest first.

## November 2026

### Trending coins from everywhere

The coins trending on Solana and our other chains now show on Coins → All and in Trending, next to coins launched here. They're picked by metrics chosen to keep out as much nonsense as possible, and Solana comes first. New, Graduating and Graduated stay ours only. See [External tokens](external-tokens.md).

### Trade external coins

External coins have the same kind of page as ours, with a trade form. Solana coins trade through Jupiter. Coins on other chains can be bought with SOL, or with that chain's coin, through Relay, and arrive in your EVM wallet. Just Stealth It takes no trading fee on them.

### Where a coin comes from

On Coins and in the Feed, coins launched here carry the Stealth logo, and external coins carry their launchpad's logo. Feed posts and the Fees board show the Stealth logo on their coins too.

### Charts

- Every chart opens on **1 minute** candles. The 1 second interval is gone.
- A candle only forms when someone trades. A quiet stretch adds no empty candles, so the chart shows real activity.
- The whole history loads from the coin's launch. Scroll left for older candles.
- The $STEALTH chart now starts at its launch.
- Bubbles on the chart mark the launcher's trades, events, callouts, posts on X and your own trades. The chart's settings switch them on and off. See [The token page](token-page.md#bubbles-on-the-chart).

### A calmer Coins list

On the All list, a coin that trades nonstop can't sit at the top anymore: each coin jumps up at most once every few seconds, so other coins' trades stay visible.

### Phones

- One-tap trading: the trade sheet's **Instant** mode has a row of buy amounts and a row of sell percentages. See [Trading on phones](token-page.md#trading-on-phones).
- Opening the trade sheet brings the header and chart back into view above it.
- The bottom bar is icons only. **Home** is the Feed.

### The Feed on desktop

Three columns side by side, each one picking what it shows (the timeline, posts, coins, Trending, top launchers, fees), or one wide column with a fuller side rail. Your choice is remembered.

### Portfolio

A cleaner Portfolio: one Connect, your holdings only when a wallet is connected, and the $STEALTH tiles between Holdings and Earnings.

### $STEALTH

- Buy-backs of $STEALTH are paid for by the coin's own fees. What's bought is burned, and part is set aside for future staking rewards. Each one shows on the coin's page with its transaction.
- The $STEALTH page links to its CoinGecko page.
- [Staking](staking.md) is built and tested, and opens soon.

### Chains

Avalanche, Arbitrum and X Layer show as coming soon in Connect, on Coins and in the launch flow.

### Everywhere

Links to our GitHub, X, Instagram and Telegram are in the header, the Connect window and the footer.

### Fees

There is no trading fee from Just Stealth It. A trading fee will be introduced later, to fund referral rewards and callouts, which are being built now. See [Fees](fees.md#trading-fees).
